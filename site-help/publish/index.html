Get writing Ready and publish

Strange Country uses three states for Posts and Misc Musings:

  • Private — not publicly visible.
  • Ready to publish — prepared for the next publication, but still not live.
  • On website — currently public.

Mark a Post or Misc Musing as Ready to publish

When a new Post or Misc Musing is finished and should be included the next time you publish, open Terminal and enter:

strange-country ready

Press Return.

Type one or more distinctive words from the title and press Return. If several pieces match, type the number beside the correct one and press Return.

If you know the exact title, you can identify the piece directly:

strange-country ready "My Exact Title"

Press Return. If the title matches exactly, Strange Country skips the numbered selection.

Strange Country shows the selected piece and asks whether to mark it Ready. Check the title, type:

yes

and press Return.

Marking a piece Ready does not put it on the live website. It simply tells Strange Country that the piece may be included when you next publish.

If you change your mind

To return a Ready to publish piece to Private before it has been published, enter:

strange-country make-private

Press Return.

Find the piece in the same way as above. If you know the exact title, you can instead enter:

strange-country make-private "My Exact Title"

Press Return.

Check the selected piece, type yes, and press Return. The piece returns to Private and will not be included the next time you publish.

Publish your changes

When a Post or Misc Musing is Ready to publish, you can send it to the live website.

Open Terminal and enter:

strange-country publish

Press Return.

Strange Country shows the changes waiting to be published. These may include:

  • new Posts or Misc Musings that are Ready to publish;
  • changes to writing already On website;
  • changes to About.

Private writing that is not Ready stays private.

If you want to publish one particular waiting change and know its exact title, you can identify it directly:

strange-country publish "My Exact Title"

Press Return. If the title matches exactly, Strange Country goes directly to the publication review for that change.

Otherwise, Terminal gives you three choices:

  1. publish all waiting changes;
  2. choose which changes to publish;
  3. cancel.

Type the number for the option you want and press Return.

If you choose particular changes, Terminal shows a numbered list. Type one number, or several numbers separated by commas, and press Return.

Strange Country then shows the final list and asks:

Publish these changes?

Check the list carefully. Type yes and press Return.

Keep Terminal open while Strange Country sends the changes to the live website.

When publication is complete, Terminal will say:

YOUR CHANGES ARE NOW ON THE WEBSITE

The public website will open in your browser.

Edit and republish existing writing

Edit a Post or Misc Musing that is already On website in iA Writer as usual. iA Writer saves the edit on this computer, but the current live version remains unchanged until you publish again.

Preview the change if you want to check it first.

When you are ready, enter:

strange-country publish

Press Return.

The changed piece appears among the waiting changes. You do not need to mark an already-published piece Ready again.

Follow the normal publication steps above.

Remove a published piece from the website

If you want to take a Post or Misc Musing off the live website without deleting your writing, use Unpublish.

Open Terminal and enter:

strange-country unpublish

Press Return.

Type one or more distinctive words from the title and press Return. If several pieces match, type the number beside the correct one and press Return.

If you know the exact title, you can instead enter:

strange-country unpublish "My Exact Title"

Press Return.

Strange Country shows the selected piece and explains that it will be removed from the live website but kept privately. Check the title, type yes, and press Return.

Keep Terminal open while the removal is completed.

Once Strange Country confirms the removal, the piece is no longer public. Its writing and images remain privately and can be published again later.
