Formatting scripts and screenplays

This guide shows how to format scripts and screenplays for publication on Strange Country.

It follows familiar screenplay conventions for scene headings, action, dialogue, parentheticals, and transitions, while keeping the writing in Markdown.

It is designed for clear publication on the website, not for preparing a screenplay for formal submission to a studio or production company.

Scene headings

Begin each new scene with a second-level heading in capitals.

Use INT. for an interior scene and EXT. for an exterior scene, followed by the location and time of day:

## INT. HOTEL CORRIDOR — NIGHT

## EXT. RAILWAY PLATFORM — DAWN

Keep the order: interior or exterior → location → time of day.

Action

Write action as ordinary paragraphs, without any special Markdown formatting:

Mara stops outside the last door. Music is playing somewhere below.

Leave one completely empty line between separate action paragraphs.

Character names and dialogue

Put the character’s name in bold capitals. Add a backslash \ at the end of the line so the dialogue appears immediately below it:

**MARA**\
You said it was finished.

Leave one completely empty line before the next character speaks:

**MARA**\
You said it was finished.

**ELIAS**\
I said it was nearly finished.

How it appears

MARA
You said it was finished.

ELIAS
I said it was nearly finished.

Parentheticals

Put a parenthetical in italics between the character’s name and the dialogue. Add a backslash at the end of both the character-name line and the parenthetical line:

**MARA**\
*(quietly)*\
I thought you had gone.

How it appears

MARA
(quietly)
I thought you had gone.

Transitions

Put a transition in bold capitals on a line of its own:

**CUT TO:**

Other examples include:

**FADE OUT:**

**DISSOLVE TO:**

Leave one completely empty line before and after the transition.

Hard line breaks

A backslash \ at the very end of a line forces the following text onto a new line without starting a new paragraph:

**MARA**\
You said it was finished.

Do not put a space after the backslash. The backslash itself does not appear on the website.

For headings, links, images, footnotes, tables, and other Markdown formatting, see Formatting your writing.
